Shot Types: ECU to EWS

Extreme Close-Up (eyes/emotion) → Close-Up (face/reaction) → Medium Shot (dialogue) → Full Shot (body language) → Wide Shot (environment) → Extreme Wide (epic scale). Master these 6 shot sizes and you can direct any scene.

Source: Film School 101
🎬 Film School

Camera Angles & Emotional Impact

Eye-level = neutral/natural. Low angle = power/dominance. High angle = vulnerability/weakness. Dutch angle = unease/tension. Over-the-shoulder = connection between characters. Each angle tells the audience exactly how to feel.
