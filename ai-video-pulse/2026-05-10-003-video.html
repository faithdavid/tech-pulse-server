FlashVSR: Single-Step Diffusion Upscaler Brings AI-Generated Video to 4K Resolution

FlashVSR tackles a critical bottleneck in AI video production: output resolution. Most text-to-video models max out at 480p-720p, far below broadcast standards. FlashVSR uses a distilled single-step diffusion architecture that upscales AI-generated footage to 2K/4K while maintaining temporal stability and avoiding flicker. Key technical innovations include local sparse attention (efficiently handling high-res inputs without texture repetition) and a lightweight conditional decoder that references the original low-res frame during reconstruction. Native streaming support makes it practical for production pipelines.

GGBench Launches ELO-Ranked Benchmark for AI-Generated Code-Driven Graphics and Animation

GGBench introduces a novel benchmark for AI-generated graphics, testing LLMs' ability to write p5.js, Three.js, and WebGL code that renders still images and animations — rather than evaluating pixel output directly. Using an ELO ranking system (head-to-head voting on paired outputs), it compares models on code quality, motion coherence, composition, and style fidelity across categories like cityscapes, nature particles, abstract patterns, and generative art. For AI filmmakers, code-rendered graphics offer deterministic, repeatable, and cheap alternatives to video diffusion for certain production needs.
