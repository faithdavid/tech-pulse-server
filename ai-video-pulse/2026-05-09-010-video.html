Master the Shot Description Template for AI Video

Professional AI filmmakers use a two-part prompt structure: VISUAL DESCRIPTION (shot type, character details, setting, lighting, style, director influence) + ACTION DESCRIPTION (character actions, camera movement, background activity). This separates static visual elements from dynamic motion, giving AI models clear direction for both frame composition and temporal movement.

Lighting Setup Fundamentals for AI Generation

AI video models respond precisely to lighting descriptors. Soft-key lighting creates approachable, commercial looks. Dramatic lighting with cyan/magenta neon highlights evokes cyberpunk. Natural window light with warm golden tones produces intimate dialogue scenes. Specify lighting direction, quality (hard/soft), and color temperature in prompts.
